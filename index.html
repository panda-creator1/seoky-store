QUICK ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few useful details before you order. If anything is unclear, contact SEOKY on WhatsApp.

What products does SEOKY offer?

This demo storefront features everyday streetwear T-shirts in nine colors. Check the product cards for the current displayed price and available details.

How do I place an order?

Add your preferred item to the cart, continue to checkout, and submit the form to prepare an order message in WhatsApp. Your order is not automatically confirmed by the website.

Which sizes can I choose?

The demo offers sizes S, M, L and XL. Please confirm your preferred size, color and current availability with SEOKY before ordering.

Do you deliver across Sri Lanka?

The storefront advertises island-wide delivery. Delivery timing is confirmed when you contact SEOKY about your order.

Is delivery free?

This demo advertises free shipping for orders of Rs. 3,000 or more. For orders below that amount, confirm the delivery fee with SEOKY.

Can I exchange a size?

Please contact SEOKY before ordering to confirm the current size-exchange policy and any conditions.

Are stock and orders live?

No. This is a demo storefront. Stock settings are stored in the current browser only, and the website prepares a WhatsApp message draft rather than recording a confirmed order in a shared database. Please confirm availability directly.

Which promo codes are available in this demo?

SEOKY10 applies a 10% discount, and SYK55 applies a 55% discount in the current demo checkout.
